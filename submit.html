The call for abstracts for the 2027 meeting at the University of California, Davis, is open. The meeting will focus on Plato's Crito.

Please send a 500-word abstract for blind review to Carlo DaVia (cdavia@ucdavis.edu) by October 16, 2026. Full details, the refereeing timeline, and a note for anyone wishing to serve as a commentator are on the 2027 meeting page.

Questions are welcome — get in touch.
