The 2027 Meeting

The 20th annual meeting of the West Coast Plato Workshop will be held at the University of California, Davis, hosted by Carlo DaVia, Jan Szaif, and Carey Seal.

Details about the focal text for the meeting and the submission guidelines will be shared here in early fall. We hope to see you in Davis.
